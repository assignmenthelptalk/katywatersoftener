## Why Is Katy Water So Hard?

Katy water is hard because it is groundwater that has moved through mineral-rich rock.

### Where Does Katy Water Come From?

According to the City of Katy, its water is groundwater from the Gulf Coast Aquifer system, pumped through six City well plants. The Chicot and Evangeline aquifers are layers of that system, formed from marine sediments that picked up calcium and magnesium over geologic time. Katy also spans Harris, Fort Bend and Waller counties, and many addresses are served by a Municipal Utility District (MUD) that draws from the same aquifer system.

</div>

<div class="issue-item">

### How Hard Is Katy Water?

Katy water tests at a working 15 to 17 grains per gallon, about 257 to 291 mg/L <mark class="data-gap">[VERIFY: working figure, confirm against the City of Katy or your MUD's report]</mark>. The City's own report lists calcium as a secondary constituent and does not state hardness, so a tap test at your address is the reliable number.

</div>

<div class="issue-item">

### How Is Water Hardness Measured?

Hardness is measured in milligrams per liter (mg/L) or grains per gallon (gpg), where 1 gpg equals about 17.1 mg/L. The U.S. Geological Survey rates water under 60 mg/L as soft, 61 to 120 mg/L as moderately hard, 121 to 180 mg/L as hard and anything over 180 mg/L as very hard. By that scale, Katy water at a working 15 to 17 gpg tests very hard.

</div>

<div class="issue-item">

### What Does Hard Water Do to a Home?

Hard water leaves marks across the whole house. Scale builds inside water heaters, dishwashers and washing machines, narrows pipes, clogs showerheads and leaves white crust on faucets. Hard water also reacts with soap to form a film that leaves skin dry and hair dull, and minerals stiffen laundry. Newer homes with tankless water heaters and high-end dishwashers see the damage in dollars.

</div>

<div class="issue-item">

### How Do You Find Your Exact Hardness?

A free water test shows the exact reading at your tap, because the water provider and your home plumbing both move the number. The technician records it in grains per gallon and sizes the softener to that figure, whichever district serves your address.

</div>

<div class="issue-item">

### How Does a Water Softener Fix Hard Water?

A water softener removes calcium and magnesium through ion exchange and replaces them with sodium or potassium, so scale stops forming and soap lathers properly. For Katy homeowners, that single change protects the water heater, the plumbing and the fixtures at the same time.
