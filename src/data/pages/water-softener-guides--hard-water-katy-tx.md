## What Do Katy Homeowners Need to Know About Hard Water?

Three questions cover the basics: what hard water is, why Katy water is hard and how hard it measures.

<div class="svc-grid">

<div class="svc-card">


![What Is Hard Water? Katy TX](/placeholders/card.svg)

### What Is Hard Water?

Hard water contains dissolved calcium and magnesium. It is measured in milligrams per liter (mg/L) or grains per gallon (gpg), where 1 gpg equals about 17.1 mg/L. The USGS rates water under 60 mg/L as soft, 61 to 120 mg/L as moderately hard, 121 to 180 mg/L as hard and over 180 mg/L as very hard.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Why Is Katy Water Hard? Katy TX](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Why Is Katy Water Hard?

Katy water is hard because it is groundwater. The Chicot and Evangeline aquifers, layers of the Gulf Coast Aquifer system, formed from marine sediments, and groundwater picks up calcium and magnesium as it moves through those mineral-rich formations.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![How Hard Is Katy Water? Katy TX](/placeholders/card.svg)

### How Hard Is Katy Water?

Katy water tests at a working 15 to 17 GPG, about 257 to 291 mg/L at 17.1 mg/L per gpg. <mark class="data-gap">[VERIFY: working figure, confirm against the City of Katy or your MUD's report]</mark> The City of Katy's own report lists calcium as a secondary constituent and does not state hardness, and homes in a MUD can differ, so your tap reading decides.

</div>

</div>

## What Hard Water Does to a Home

Hard water leaves scale in water heaters, dishwashers, washing machines, pipes and fixtures, and it reacts with soap to leave a film on skin, hair and laundry. Scale narrows pipes over time and makes appliances work harder.

## Solutions for Hard Water

A [salt-based](/water-softener-guides/salt-vs-salt-free-water-softener/) water softener removes the hardness minerals, a [salt-free conditioner](/salt-free-water-softener-systems/) changes how scale forms, and a filter does not address hardness. For the strongest result in water above 180 mg/L, a correctly sized salt-based softener is the standard answer. For installation, see [Water Softener Installation in Katy, TX](/water-softener-installation/).

> **Not a good fit if...** your tap tests under 60 mg/L. That water is soft, and a softener adds no benefit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<div class="faq-list">

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Is Katy water hard?</summary>

Yes. Katy water tests at a working 15 to 17 GPG, which is Very Hard on the WQA scale above 10.5 GPG, so scale builds quickly in water heaters and on fixtures.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Is hard water bad for your health?</summary>

Hard water is not a health risk in the amounts found in Katy. It affects plumbing, appliances, skin and laundry rather than drinking safety.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Why is my neighbor's water harder than mine?</summary>

Many Katy addresses are served by Municipal Utility Districts rather than the City of Katy directly, and the pipes in your home change what reaches the tap. A test shows your reading.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Does boiling remove water hardness?</summary>

Boiling precipitates some of the temporary hardness as scale, and it does not make hard water soft. A softener removes the minerals before they reach the pipes.
