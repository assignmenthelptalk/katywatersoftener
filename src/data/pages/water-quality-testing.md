## What Does Water Quality Testing Measure in Katy, TX?

Seven tests cover hardness, total dissolved solids, iron, pH, chlorine, sulfur and heavy metals.

<div class="svc-grid">

<div class="svc-card">


![Free Hard Water Test Katy TX](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Free Hard Water Test

A [hard water](/water-softener-guides/hard-water-katy-tx/) test reads calcium and magnesium in grains per gallon. Katy water works out at a working 15 to 17 gpg, and the test shows your home's number so a softener is sized correctly.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![TDS Testing Katy TX](/placeholders/card.svg)

### TDS Testing

Total dissolved solids measures all dissolved minerals in milligrams per liter. A high reading points to [reverse osmosis](/reverse-osmosis-installation/) or another treatment for drinking water.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Iron Content Testing Katy TX](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Iron Content Testing

Iron stains fixtures and laundry and fouls softener resin. A reading above the softener's limit means an iron filter should go in first.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![pH Level Testing Katy TX](/placeholders/card.svg)

### pH Level Testing

pH shows whether water is acidic or alkaline. Low pH corrodes pipes, and the reading guides material and treatment choices.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Chlorine Testing Katy TX](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Chlorine Testing

Chlorine is the disinfectant in utility water. A tap test shows the level at your home, which affects taste and odor.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Sulfur Testing Katy TX](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Sulfur Testing

Sulfur gives a rotten-egg smell, and it is mainly a [private well](/well-water-softener-katy-tx/) issue. A positive test points to a hydrogen sulfide filter.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Heavy Metals Testing Katy TX](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Heavy Metals Testing
