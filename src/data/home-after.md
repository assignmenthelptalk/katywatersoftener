<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Salt vs. salt-free water softener: what is the difference?</summary>

A salt-based softener removes calcium and magnesium by ion exchange and leaves soft water. A salt-free conditioner changes how scale forms but leaves the minerals in the water. Choose salt-based for the strongest result in very hard water, and salt-free if you want no salt or brine drain and accept a milder effect.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>How long does water softener installation take?</summary>

A standard installation typically takes about 2 to 4 hours. Plumbing access, drain location and system type can lengthen the visit, and the technician confirms the schedule at your estimate.

</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Do I need a water softener with city water or well water in Katy?</summary>

Yes, in both cases, because hardness appears in groundwater whether it arrives through a City or MUD line or a private well. Private wells need a water test first for iron and sulfur, which affect the softener.

</details>

<summary>How often does a water softener need maintenance?</summary>
